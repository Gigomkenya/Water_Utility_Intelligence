## Customer Dataset – Data Quality Report

**Source:** Mockaroo (simulated data)

**Dataset:** `customers.csv`

**Number of records:** 5,000

### Validation performed
- Checked for complete duplicate records.
- Checked for duplicate customer IDs.
- Assigned unique sequential customer IDs to the simulated customer records.
- Preserved the original customer records during validation.

### Validation outcome
- Complete duplicate records: 0
- Duplicate customer IDs: 0
- Unique customer IDs: 5,000

### Final Customer Dataset

**Dataset:** `customers_final.csv`

The final customer dataset contains 5,000 simulated customer records and is prepared for analysis in Power BI. It includes customer demographics, contact fields, customer categories, county, service area, connection dates and connection status.
